About

I’m a PhD researcher in Psychology at IIT (BHU), Varanasi, working at the intersection of social cognition, misinformation, and artificial intelligence. My research explores how people form and evaluate beliefs, why misinformation persists, and how AI-driven interventions can improve our ability to distinguish information from misinformation. I’m particularly interested in misinformation interventions,human cognition, social psychology, experimental methods, and computational approaches to human behaviour.
